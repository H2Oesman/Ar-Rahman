70,000 tents. One night of rain.

“Those who spend their wealth by night and day, by stealth and openly, verily their reward is with their Lord, and there shall no fear come upon them neither shall they grieve.”– Qur'an, Al-Baqarah 2:274

These families lost their homes long ago. Now the rain is taking their tents. Mattresses soaked through. Blankets that won’t dry. Children shivering in wet clothes through the night. Fathers digging trenches with their bare hands to turn the water away.

And winter has barely begun.

Our verified local partners are on the ground right now, and our emergency response there has already reached more than 500,000 people. Every family is checked against verified lists, so your gift reaches the family it’s meant for.

Etiquettes of traveling — what to settle before you leave, what to do on the day, and the du‘ās to recite as you set off.

Before the Journey

1. Tawbah & settling rights Make tawbah (repentance) for all your sins. Return what belongs to others by settling your debts and handing back any trusts (amānah) left in your care, and ask forgiveness from anyone you have wronged.
2. Your will (waṣiyyah) Leave your waṣiyyah (will) with someone you trust.
3. Provide for your family Make sure your family has everything they need during your absence.
4. Call your parents Call your parents, ask their forgiveness, and ask them to make du‘ā’ for you.
5. Print your visa and e-ticket Your visa has been emailed to you. Print a copy of your visa and your flight e-ticket and bring them with you, and set up the Nusuk app before you leave (see “In Madinah” below).
6. Join the WhatsApp group The group will use WhatsApp for communication and updates throughout the Umrah. Join the WhatsApp group.
Note: from Friday, 13 November, all trip information will be sent through this WhatsApp group.

Before Setting Off from Home

Ghusl Perform ghusl (a full-body ritual wash). This is the recommended ghusl for travel. The ghusl for iḥrām comes later, in Madinah, before you enter iḥrām for Umrah.
Grooming Trim your nails and remove pubic hair.
Pray what is due If a ṣalāh is due when you are about to leave, make sure you have prayed it in full before setting off.
Combine your prayers Combine your prayers (for example, Ẓuhr with ‘Aṣr, or Maghrib with ‘Ishā’) at home or at the airport.
Two rak‘āt before leaving Pray two rak‘āt at home before you leave. See the du‘ā’ section below.

Ṣalāh & Du‘ās for Departure

Step 1 | Two rak‘āt at home
First rak‘ah
سُورَةُ ٱلْكَافِرُونَ
Sūrat al-Kāfirūn
Second rak‘ah
سُورَةُ ٱلْإِخْلَاصِ
Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ
After al-Fātiḥah, recite al-Kāfirūn in the first rak‘ah and al-Ikhlāṣ in the second.
Step 2 | Farewell du‘ā’ — said by the traveller
Say this to your family and to those who come to say goodbye to you.
أَسْتَوْدِعُكُمُ ٱللَّهَ ٱلَّذِي لَا تَضِيعُ وَدَائِعُهُ
Astawdi‘ukumullāhalladhī lā taḍī‘u wadā’i‘uh.
I entrust you to Allah, whose trusts are never lost.
Ibn Mājah 2825; Aḥmad
Step 3 | Farewell du‘ā’ — said to the traveller
Your family and those seeing you off say this to you.
أَسْتَوْدِعُ ٱللَّهَ دِينَكَ وَأَمَانَتَكَ وَخَوَاتِيمَ عَمَلِكَ، زَوَّدَكَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلتَّقْوَىٰ، وَغَفَرَ ذَنْبَكَ، وَيَسَّرَ لَكَ ٱلْخَيْرَ حَيْثُمَا كُنْتَ
Astawdi‘ullāha dīnaka wa amānataka wa khawātīma ‘amalik. Zawwadakallāhut-taqwā, wa ghafara dhanbak, wa yassara lakal-khayra ḥaythumā kunt.
I entrust your religion, your trust, and the end of your deeds to Allah. May Allah provide you with taqwā, forgive your sins, and make all that is good easy for you wherever you are.
Abū Dāwūd 2600; at-Tirmidhī 3442, 3444
Step 4 | Leaving the house
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ، تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ، وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِٱللَّهِ
Bismillāh, tawakkaltu ‘alallāh, wa lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā billāh.
In the name of Allah, I place my trust in Allah, and there is no might or power except with Allah.
Abū Dāwūd 5095; at-Tirmidhī 3426

What is the Rawdah?

The Rawdah (ar-Rawḍah ash-Sharīfah) is a small area inside Masjid an-Nabawī, between the house of the Prophet ﷺ, where he is buried, and his minbar. It is marked by a green carpet. Visitors pray two rak‘āt there and make du‘ā’. Visits are usually booked in advance and are limited in time, with separate slots for men and women, so stay calm and give others room.

مَا بَيْنَ بَيْتِي وَمِنْبَرِي رَوْضَةٌ مِنْ رِيَاضِ ٱلْجَنَّةِ
Mā bayna baytī wa minbarī rawḍatun min riyāḍil-jannah.
The Prophet ﷺ said: “What is between my house and my minbar is a garden from the gardens of Paradise.”
Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 1196; Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 1391

Interesting information: Ṣalāt al-Jumu‘ah in Madinah Our Jumu‘ah in Madinah falls on Friday, 27 November 2026. Masjid an-Nabawī fills up early, and once it reaches capacity, entry to the indoor prayer halls is closed.

If you are already inside and need to step out for wuḍū’ or another need, ask the door attendant for a Tasheel Card (Facilitation Card, بطاقة تسهيل, “exit and return of the worshipper when needed”) before you leave. Show it at the entrance when you come back, and you will be let in even if general entry has been closed, so you don’t lose your place.

Tip: arrive early, make wuḍū’ before you come, and keep the card safe until you are back inside.

Apa itu Raudah?

Raudah (Ar-Raudah Asy-Syarifah) adalah area kecil di dalam Masjid Nabawi, di antara rumah Rasulullah ﷺ, tempat beliau dimakamkan, dan mimbar beliau. Area ini ditandai dengan karpet berwarna hijau. Para peziarah salat dua rakaat dan berdoa di sana. Kunjungan biasanya dipesan terlebih dahulu dan waktunya terbatas, dengan jadwal terpisah untuk laki-laki dan perempuan, jadi tetaplah tenang dan berilah ruang kepada orang lain.

مَا بَيْنَ بَيْتِي وَمِنْبَرِي رَوْضَةٌ مِنْ رِيَاضِ ٱلْجَنَّةِ
Ma baina baiti wa minbari raudhatun min riyadhil jannah.
Rasulullah ﷺ bersabda: “Antara rumahku dan mimbarku adalah salah satu taman dari taman-taman surga.”
HR. Bukhari 1196; Muslim 1391
