Du‘ās for every step of Umrah, in the order you will need them: from leaving home and Madinah, through iḥrām, ṭawāf round by round and sa‘ī, to returning home. Drawn from three guides: the Book of Du‘a, Do’a Tawaf, and the Sa’i guide.

Where a guide gives only the Arabic, the transliteration and translation have been added here. The round-by-round ṭawāf du‘ās and the du‘ā’ after sa‘ī are compiled du‘ās, not fixed by a ḥadīth; any du‘ā’ from the heart, in any language, is welcome there. References were checked against the ḥadīth collections (sunnah.com and Ḥiṣn al-Muslim); where a wording could not be traced to a source, its card says so. Qur’ān text: Uthmānī script. Audio: Ḥiṣn al-Muslim (hisnmuslim.com) and Mishary Alafasy.

Before Setting Out

Two rak‘āt at home before leaving
Before you set off, pray two rak‘āt at home. The Prophet ﷺ is reported to have said that no one leaves behind with his family anything better than two rak‘āt prayed with them when setting out on a journey.
A good choice is al-Kāfirūn in the first rak‘ah and al-Ikhlāṣ in the second, after al-Fātiḥah. No particular sūrahs are fixed for this prayer, so any you know is fine; these two suit it well, as the Prophet ﷺ recited them in other two-rak‘ah prayers (the sunnah of Fajr, and after ṭawāf).
First rak‘ah
سُورَةُ ٱلْكَافِرُونَ
Sūrat al-Kāfirūn
Second rak‘ah
سُورَةُ ٱلْإِخْلَاصِ
Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ
Ibn Abī Shaybah, from al-Muṭ‘im ibn al-Miqdām (mursal; its chain graded ḥasan by Shaykh Muḥammad ‘Awwāmah)
Farewell du‘ā’, said by the traveller
Say this to your family and to those who come to say goodbye to you.
أَسْتَوْدِعُكُمُ ٱللَّهَ ٱلَّذِي لَا تَضِيعُ وَدَائِعُهُ
Astawdi‘ukumullāhal-ladhī lā taḍī‘u wadā’i‘uh.
I entrust you to Allah, whose trusts are never lost.
Ibn Mājah 2825; Aḥmad
Farewell du‘ā’, said to the traveller
Your family and those seeing you off say this to you, and you may say it back to them.
أَسْتَوْدِعُ ٱللَّهَ دِينَكَ وَأَمَانَتَكَ وَخَوَاتِيمَ عَمَلِكَ، زَوَّدَكَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلتَّقْوَىٰ، وَغَفَرَ ذَنْبَكَ، وَيَسَّرَ لَكَ ٱلْخَيْرَ حَيْثُمَا كُنْتَ
Astawdi‘ullāha dīnaka wa amānataka wa khawātīma ‘amalik. Zawwadakallāhut-taqwā, wa ghafara dhanbak, wa yassara lakal-khayra ḥaythumā kunt.
I entrust to Allah your religion, your trust, and the last of your deeds. May Allah provide you with taqwā, forgive your sins, and make goodness easy for you wherever you are.
Abū Dāwūd 2600; at-Tirmidhī 3443 (ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ), 3444 · Book of Du‘a p. 3
Leaving the house
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ، تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ، وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِٱللَّهِ
Bismillāh, tawakkaltu ‘alallāh, wa lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā billāh.
In the name of Allah, I place my trust in Allah, and there is no might nor power except with Allah.
Abū Dāwūd 5095; at-Tirmidhī 3426 · Book of Du‘a p. 3
Upon boarding any vehicle
For every ride after you set off (bus, train, plane): say this once you are seated. The complete travel du‘ā’ below begins with it.
سُبْحَٰنَ ٱلَّذِى سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَٰذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُۥ مُقْرِنِينَ ۝١٣ وَإِنَّآ إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا لَمُنقَلِبُونَ ۝١٤
Subḥānal-ladhī sakhkhara lanā hādhā wa mā kunnā lahū muqrinīn, wa innā ilā Rabbinā lamunqalibūn.
Glory be to the One Who has subjected this to us, for we could never have done so by ourselves. And surely to our Lord we will all return.
Az-Zukhruf 43:13–14 · Book of Du‘a p. 3
The recording is the two full verses.
Du‘ā’ for travel
Recite this before setting off in the car, after your 2 rak‘āt, or at any point on your way to the destination.
Short version
ٱللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، ٱللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، ٱللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ
سُبْحَـٰنَ ٱلَّذِى سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَـٰذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُۥ مُقْرِنِينَ ۝١٣ وَإِنَّآ إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا لَمُنقَلِبُونَ ۝١٤
Allāhu akbar, Allāhu akbar, Allāhu akbar. Subḥānalladhī sakhkhara lanā hādhā wa mā kunnā lahū muqrinīn, wa innā ilā Rabbinā lamunqalibūn.
Allah is the Greatest (×3). How perfect He is, the One who has placed this (transport) at our service, and we ourselves would not have been capable of that. And to our Lord is our final return.
Az-Zukhruf 43:13–14; Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 1342

On Arrival: a New City & Your Hotel

Entering a new town
Istanbul, Makkah, Madinah: say this on arriving in any town or city.
ٱللَّهُمَّ رَبَّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَاتِ ٱلسَّبْعِ وَمَا أَظْلَلْنَ، وَرَبَّ ٱلْأَرَضِينَ ٱلسَّبْعِ وَمَا أَقْلَلْنَ، وَرَبَّ ٱلشَّيَاطِينِ وَمَا أَضْلَلْنَ، وَرَبَّ ٱلرِّيَاحِ وَمَا ذَرَيْنَ، أَسْأَلُكَ خَيْرَ هَٰذِهِ ٱلْقَرْيَةِ وَخَيْرَ أَهْلِهَا وَخَيْرَ مَا فِيهَا، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّهَا وَشَرِّ أَهْلِهَا وَشَرِّ مَا فِيهَا
Allāhumma Rabbas-samāwātis-sab‘i wa mā aẓlalna, wa Rabbal-araḍīnas-sab‘i wa mā aqlalna, wa Rabbash-shayāṭīni wa mā aḍlalna, wa Rabbar-riyāḥi wa mā dharayna, as’aluka khayra hādhihil-qaryati wa khayra ahlihā wa khayra mā fīhā, wa a‘ūdhu bika min sharrihā wa sharri ahlihā wa sharri mā fīhā.
O Allah, Lord of the seven heavens and all they shade, Lord of the seven earths and all they carry, Lord of the devils and all they misguide, Lord of the winds and all they scatter: I ask You for the good of this town, the good of its people, and the good of all that is in it; and I seek refuge in You from its evil, the evil of its people, and the evil of all that is in it.
Al-Ḥākim 2/100 (graded ṣaḥīḥ, adh-Dhahabī agreed); Ibn as-Sunnī 524; an-Nasā’ī (al-Kubrā) · Book of Du‘a p. 5
Entering the hotel / new lodging
Whoever stops at a place and says this, nothing will harm him until he leaves that place.
أَعُوذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلتَّامَّاتِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ
A‘ūdhu bi-kalimātillāhit-tāmmāti min sharri mā khalaq.
I seek refuge in the Perfect Words of Allah from the evil of what He has created.
Muslim 2708 · Book of Du‘a p. 6

In Madinah

The Rawḍah
The Rawḍah (ar-Rawḍah ash-Sharīfah) is a small area inside Masjid an-Nabawī, between the house of the Prophet ﷺ, where he is buried, and his minbar. It is marked by a green carpet. Visitors pray two rak‘āt there and make du‘ā’. Visits are usually booked in advance (Nusuk app) and are limited in time, with separate slots for men and women, so stay calm and give others room.
مَا بَيْنَ بَيْتِي وَمِنْبَرِي رَوْضَةٌ مِنْ رِيَاضِ ٱلْجَنَّةِ
Mā bayna baytī wa minbarī rawḍatun min riyāḍil-jannah.
The Prophet ﷺ said: “What is between my house and my minbar is a garden from the gardens of Paradise.”
Al-Bukhārī 1196; Muslim 1391

Entering Iḥrām

Intention for Umrah
At or before the mīqāt, in iḥrām, make the intention for Umrah:
لَبَّيْكَ ٱللَّهُمَّ بِعُمْرَةٍ
ٱللَّهُمَّ مَحِلِّي حَيْثُ حَبَسْتَنِي
Labbayk Allāhumma bi-‘umrah. Allāhumma maḥillī ḥaythu ḥabastanī.
Here I am, O Allah, for Umrah. (Condition:) O Allah, my place of leaving iḥrām is wherever You hold me back.
The second line is an optional condition (ishtirāṭ): if illness or an obstacle stops you from completing the Umrah, you may leave iḥrām there. The Prophet ﷺ taught it to Ḍubā‘ah bint az-Zubayr when she was unwell. Scholars differ on whether it suits every pilgrim or only those who fear being prevented, so follow the advice of your group’s scholar.
Al-Bukhārī 5089; Muslim 1207 · Book of Du‘a p. 4
Talbiyah for Umrah
Men recite it aloud; women softly. Keep repeating it on the way to Makkah. For Umrah, the talbiyah stops when you begin ṭawāf.
لَبَّيْكَ ٱللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ ٱلْحَمْدَ وَٱلنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَٱلْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ
Labbayk Allāhumma labbayk, labbayka lā sharīka laka labbayk, innal-ḥamda wan-ni‘mata laka wal-mulk, lā sharīka lak.
Here I am, O Allah, here I am. Here I am, You have no partner, here I am. Surely all praise and blessings are Yours, and the dominion. You have no partner.
Al-Bukhārī 1549; Muslim 1184 · Book of Du‘a p. 4

Entering Masjid al-Ḥarām
Enter with your right foot. Keep your slippers in a bag.
ٱللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍ وَسَلِّمْ
ٱللَّهُمَّ ٱفْتَحْ لِي أَبْوَابَ رَحْمَتِكَ
Allāhumma ṣalli ‘alā Muḥammadin wa sallim. Allāhummaf-taḥ lī abwāba raḥmatik.
O Allah, send blessings and peace upon Muḥammad. O Allah, open the doors of Your mercy for me.
Muslim 713; Abū Dāwūd 465 · Book of Du‘a p. 6

Ṭawāf

How to use the round-by-round du‘ās

Make your intention for seven rounds, keeping the Ka‘bah on your left. The du‘ās that follow, one for each round, are read from the Black Stone to the Yemeni Corner; between the Yemeni Corner and the Black Stone, every round ends with Rabbanā ātinā.

These round du‘ās are a compiled tradition, widely used in Indonesian manasik guides; the Sunnah does not fix a specific du‘ā’ for each round. The one du‘ā’ reported from the Prophet ﷺ in ṭawāf is Rabbanā ātinā between the two corners (Abū Dāwūd 1892, ḥasan). Read them if they help you, or ask Allah for anything in your own language, from the heart. Keep your voice low so you do not disturb others.

Do’a Tawaf p. 1–2
1st round
Read from the Black Stone (Ḥajar Aswad) to the Yemeni Corner (Rukn Yamānī).
سُبْحَانَ ٱللَّهِ وَٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ وَلَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ وَٱللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِٱللَّهِ ٱلْعَلِيِّ ٱلْعَظِيمِ، وَٱلصَّلَاةُ وَٱلسَّلَامُ عَلَىٰ رَسُولِ ٱللَّهِ صَلَّى ٱللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ. ٱللَّهُمَّ إِيمَانًا بِكَ، وَتَصْدِيقًا بِكِتَابِكَ، وَوَفَاءً بِعَهْدِكَ، وَٱتِّبَاعًا لِسُنَّةِ نَبِيِّكَ مُحَمَّدٍ صَلَّى ٱللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ. ٱللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ ٱلْعَفْوَ وَٱلْعَافِيَةَ وَٱلْمُعَافَاةَ ٱلدَّائِمَةَ فِي ٱلدِّينِ وَٱلدُّنْيَا وَٱلْآخِرَةِ، وَٱلْفَوْزَ بِٱلْجَنَّةِ وَٱلنَّجَاةَ مِنَ ٱلنَّارِ
Subḥānallāhi wal-ḥamdu lillāhi wa lā ilāha illallāhu wallāhu akbar, wa lā ḥawla wa lā quwwata illā billāhil-‘aliyyil-‘aẓīm, waṣ-ṣalātu was-salāmu ‘alā Rasūlillāhi ṣallallāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam. Allāhumma īmānan bik, wa taṣdīqan bi-kitābik, wa wafā’an bi-‘ahdik, wattibā‘an li-sunnati nabiyyika Muḥammadin ṣallallāhu ‘alayhi wa sallam. Allāhumma innī as’alukal-‘afwa wal-‘āfiyata wal-mu‘āfātad-dā’imata fid-dīni wad-dunyā wal-ākhirah, wal-fawza bil-jannati wan-najāta minan-nār.
Glory be to Allah, all praise is for Allah, there is no god but Allah, and Allah is the Greatest; there is no might nor power except with Allah, the Most High, the Almighty. Blessings and peace be upon the Messenger of Allah ﷺ. O Allah, (I do this) out of faith in You, belief in Your Book, faithfulness to Your covenant, and following the Sunnah of Your Prophet Muḥammad ﷺ. O Allah, I ask You for pardon, well-being and lasting protection in religion, in this world and in the Hereafter, and for success in attaining Paradise and salvation from the Fire.
Do’a Tawaf p. 1
2nd round
Read from the Black Stone (Ḥajar Aswad) to the Yemeni Corner (Rukn Yamānī).
ٱللَّهُمَّ إِنَّ هَٰذَا ٱلْبَيْتَ بَيْتُكَ، وَٱلْحَرَمَ حَرَمُكَ، وَٱلْأَمْنَ أَمْنُكَ، وَٱلْعَبْدَ عَبْدُكَ، وَأَنَا عَبْدُكَ وَٱبْنُ عَبْدِكَ، وَهَٰذَا مَقَامُ ٱلْعَائِذِ بِكَ مِنَ ٱلنَّارِ، فَحَرِّمْ لُحُومَنَا وَبَشَرَتَنَا عَلَى ٱلنَّارِ. ٱللَّهُمَّ حَبِّبْ إِلَيْنَا ٱلْإِيمَانَ وَزَيِّنْهُ فِي قُلُوبِنَا، وَكَرِّهْ إِلَيْنَا ٱلْكُفْرَ وَٱلْفُسُوقَ وَٱلْعِصْيَانَ، وَٱجْعَلْنَا مِنَ ٱلرَّاشِدِينَ. ٱللَّهُمَّ قِنِي عَذَابَكَ يَوْمَ تَبْعَثُ عِبَادَكَ. ٱللَّهُمَّ ٱرْزُقْنِيَ ٱلْجَنَّةَ بِغَيْرِ حِسَابٍ
Allāhumma inna hādhal-bayta baytuk, wal-ḥarama ḥaramuk, wal-amna amnuk, wal-‘abda ‘abduk, wa ana ‘abduka wabnu ‘abdik, wa hādhā maqāmul-‘ā’idhi bika minan-nār, fa-ḥarrim luḥūmanā wa basharatanā ‘alan-nār. At the Black Stone and the Yemeni Corner
At the Black Stone, at the start of every round, face it and say takbīr. The Prophet ﷺ said takbīr each time he came to its corner (al-Bukhārī 1613).
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ، ٱللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ
Bismillāh, Allāhu akbar.
In the name of Allah; Allah is the Greatest.
At the Yemeni Corner, the Sunnah is to touch it with your right hand if you can reach it (al-Bukhārī 1609); if you cannot, pass on without gesturing. Your Book of Du‘a also places these words at the Yemeni Corner. Scholars differ on whether takbīr is said there, especially when you cannot touch it, so follow the guidance of your group’s scholar.

Sa‘ī between Ṣafā and Marwah

What is Sa‘ī? How it works
  • Starting point: it always begins at the hill of Ṣafā and finishes at Marwah.
  • Counting laps: each one-way trip is one lap (Ṣafā to Marwah is lap 1, Marwah to Ṣafā is lap 2, and so on), seven laps in all.
  • Distance: the hills are about 450 metres apart, so the seven laps cover about 3.15 km (1.96 miles).
  • The green markers: between Ṣafā and Marwah you pass two sets of green fluorescent lights. Between them it is sunnah for men to jog or walk at a brisk pace; women continue at a normal pace.
Sa’i guide
Approaching Ṣafā (and Marwah)
Recite the verse as you approach Ṣafā; the Sa’i guide repeats it on approaching Marwah too. At Ṣafā, the first time only, add “I begin with what Allah began with.”
إِنَّ ٱلصَّفَا وَٱلْمَرْوَةَ مِن شَعَآئِرِ ٱللَّهِ ۖ فَمَنْ حَجَّ ٱلْبَيْتَ أَوِ ٱعْتَمَرَ فَلَا جُنَاحَ عَلَيْهِ أَن يَطَّوَّفَ بِهِمَا ۚ وَمَن تَطَوَّعَ خَيْرًا فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ شَاكِرٌ عَلِيمٌ ۝١٥٨
أَبْدَأُ بِمَا بَدَأَ ٱللَّهُ بِهِ
Innaṣ-Ṣafā wal-Marwata min sha‘ā’irillāh, fa-man ḥajjal-bayta awi‘tamara falā junāḥa ‘alayhi an yaṭṭawwafa bihimā, wa man taṭawwa‘a khayran fa-innallāha shākirun ‘alīm. Abda’u bimā bada’allāhu bih.
Indeed, Ṣafā and Marwah are among the symbols of Allah. So whoever performs Hajj to the House or Umrah, there is no blame on him for walking between them. And whoever does good voluntarily, then indeed Allah is Appreciative and All-Knowing. I begin with what Allah began with.
Al-Baqarah 2:158; Muslim 1218 · Sa’i guide · Book of Du‘a p. 9

Climb until you can see the Ka‘bah, face it, raise your hands, and say:
ٱللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، ٱللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، ٱللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ
لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ ٱلْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ ٱلْحَمْدُ، وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ، لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ، أَنْجَزَ وَعْدَهُ، وَنَصَرَ عَبْدَهُ، وَهَزَمَ ٱلْأَحْزَابَ وَحْدَهُ
Allāhu akbar, Allāhu akbar, Allāhu akbar. Lā ilāha illallāhu waḥdahū lā sharīka lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-ḥamd, wa huwa ‘alā kulli shay’in qadīr. Lā ilāha illallāhu waḥdah, anjaza wa‘dah, wa naṣara ‘abdah, wa hazamal-aḥzāba waḥdah.
Allah is the Greatest (three times). There is no god but Allah alone, without partner. To Him belongs the dominion and all praise, and He has power over all things. There is no god but Allah alone. He fulfilled His promise, supported His servant, and defeated the confederates alone.
Then make du‘ā’. Repeat this on every arrival at Ṣafā or Marwah.
Muslim 1218 · Book of Du‘a p. 9
The recording is Jābir’s full narration of the Prophet’s ﷺ sa‘ī, which contains this dhikr.
Do’a Sa‘ī (fuller form)
A fuller dhikr from the Sa’i guide, for the hills of Ṣafā and Marwah:
ٱللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ ٱللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ ٱللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ وَلِلَّهِ ٱلْحَمْدُ. ٱللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ عَلَىٰ مَا هَدَانَا، وَٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ عَلَىٰ مَا أَوْلَانَا. لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ ٱلْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ ٱلْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ. أَنْجَزَ وَعْدَهُ، وَنَصَرَ عَبْدَهُ، وَهَزَمَ ٱلْأَحْزَابَ وَحْدَهُ. لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ وَلَا نَعْبُدُ إِلَّا إِيَّاهُ، مُخْلِصِينَ لَهُ ٱلدِّينَ وَلَوْ كَرِهَ ٱلْكَافِرُونَ. وَٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَالَمِينَ
Allāhu akbar, Allāhu akbar, Allāhu akbar, wa lillāhil-ḥamd. Allāhu akbaru ‘alā mā hadānā, wal-ḥamdu lillāhi ‘alā mā awlānā. Lā ilāha illallāhu waḥdahū lā sharīka lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-ḥamdu wa huwa ‘alā kulli shay’in qadīr. Anjaza wa‘dah, wa naṣara ‘abdah, wa hazamal-aḥzāba waḥdah. Lā ilāha illallāhu wa lā na‘budu illā iyyāh, mukhliṣīna lahud-dīna wa law karihal-kāfirūn. Wal-ḥamdu lillāhi Rabbil-‘ālamīn.
Allah is the Greatest, Allah is the Greatest, Allah is the Greatest, and to Allah belongs all praise. Allah is the Greatest for guiding us, and all praise is for Allah for what He has bestowed on us. There is no god but Allah alone, without partner; to Him belongs the dominion and all praise, and He has power over all things. He fulfilled His promise, supported His servant, and defeated the confederates alone. There is no god but Allah, and we worship none but Him, making our religion sincerely His, even though the disbelievers dislike it. And all praise is for Allah, Lord of the worlds.
Its central lines are the Prophet’s ﷺ dhikr on Ṣafā (Muslim 1218). I could not verify a single source for this full wording.
Sa’i guide
Between the green lights
Men jog lightly between the two green lights; women walk at a normal pace. Recite:
رَبِّ ٱغْفِرْ وَٱرْحَمْ، وَٱعْفُ وَتَكَرَّمْ، وَتَجَاوَزْ عَمَّا تَعْلَمُ، إِنَّكَ تَعْلَمُ مَا لَا نَعْلَمُ. ٱللَّهُمَّ ٱغْفِرْ وَٱرْحَمْ، إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ ٱلْأَعَزُّ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ. رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي ٱلدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي ٱلْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ ٱلنَّارِ
Rabbighfir warḥam, wa‘fu wa takarram, wa tajāwaz ‘ammā ta‘lam, innaka ta‘lamu mā lā na‘lam. Allāhummaghfir warḥam, innaka antal-a‘azzul-akram. Rabbanā ātinā fid-dunyā ḥasanatan wa fil-ākhirati ḥasanatan wa qinā ‘adhāban-nār.
My Lord, forgive and have mercy, pardon and be generous, and overlook what You know (of my sins); surely You know what we do not know. O Allah, forgive and have mercy; surely You are the Most Mighty, the Most Noble. Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and save us from the torment of the Fire.
The core words, Rabbighfir warḥam, innaka antal-a‘azzul-akram, are reported from ‘Abdullāh ibn Mas‘ūd and Ibn ‘Umar during sa‘ī (Muṣannaf Ibn Abī Shaybah). The added phrases appear in later du‘ā’ books.

Du‘ā’ after Sa‘ī
After the seventh lap, ending at Marwah:
ٱللَّهُمَّ رَبَّنَا تَقَبَّلْ مِنَّا، وَعَافِنَا وَٱعْفُ عَنَّا، وَعَلَىٰ طَاعَتِكَ وَشُكْرِكَ أَعِنَّا، وَعَلَىٰ غَيْرِكَ لَا تَكِلْنَا، وَعَلَى ٱلْإِيمَانِ وَٱلْإِسْلَامِ ٱلْكَامِلِ جَمِيعًا تَوَفَّنَا وَأَنْتَ رَاضٍ عَنَّا. ٱللَّهُمَّ ٱرْحَمْنِي بِتَرْكِ ٱلْمَعَاصِي أَبَدًا مَا أَبْقَيْتَنِي، وَٱرْحَمْنِي أَنْ أَتَكَلَّفَ مَا لَا يَعْنِينِي، وَٱرْزُقْنِي حُسْنَ ٱلنَّظَرِ فِيمَا يُرْضِيكَ عَنِّي، يَا أَرْحَمَ ٱلرَّاحِمِينَ
Allāhumma Rabbanā taqabbal minnā, wa ‘āfinā wa‘fu ‘annā, wa ‘alā ṭā‘atika wa shukrika a‘innā, wa ‘alā ghayrika lā takilnā, wa ‘alal-īmāni wal-islāmil-kāmili jamī‘an tawaffanā wa anta rāḍin ‘annā. Allāhummarḥamnī bi-tarkil-ma‘āṣī abadan mā abqaytanī, warḥamnī an atakallafa mā lā ya‘nīnī, warzuqnī ḥusnan-naẓari fīmā yurḍīka ‘annī, yā Arḥamar-rāḥimīn.
O Allah, our Lord, accept from us, grant us well-being and pardon us; help us to obey You and to be grateful to You; do not leave us to anyone other than You; and cause us all to die upon complete faith and Islam while You are pleased with us. O Allah, have mercy on me by (helping me) leave sins forever, as long as You keep me alive; have mercy on me so that I do not burden myself with what does not concern me; and grant me good insight into what makes You pleased with me, O Most Merciful of the merciful.
For Umrah, sa‘ī is followed by cutting or shaving the hair (taḥallul).
Sa’i guide

Du‘ās for Your Stay

Favourite du‘ā’: pardon and protection
Use your time in Makkah and Madinah, at the Multazam and after ṣalāh, for the du‘ās closest to your heart. The Prophet ﷺ never left this one, morning and evening.
ٱللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ ٱلْعَفْوَ وَٱلْعَافِيَةَ فِي ٱلدُّنْيَا وَٱلْآخِرَةِ، ٱللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ ٱلْعَفْوَ وَٱلْعَافِيَةَ فِي دِينِي وَدُنْيَايَ وَأَهْلِي وَمَالِي، ٱللَّهُمَّ ٱسْتُرْ عَوْرَاتِي، وَآمِنْ رَوْعَاتِي، ٱللَّهُمَّ ٱحْفَظْنِي مِنْ بَيْنِ يَدَيَّ، وَمِنْ خَلْفِي، وَعَنْ يَمِينِي، وَعَنْ شِمَالِي، وَمِنْ فَوْقِي، وَأَعُوذُ بِعَظَمَتِكَ أَنْ أُغْتَالَ مِنْ تَحْتِي
Allāhumma innī as’alukal-‘afwa wal-‘āfiyata fid-dunyā wal-ākhirah. Allāhumma innī as’alukal-‘afwa wal-‘āfiyata fī dīnī wa dunyāya wa ahlī wa mālī. Allāhummastur ‘awrātī, wa āmin raw‘ātī. Allāhummaḥ-faẓnī min bayni yadayya, wa min khalfī, wa ‘an yamīnī, wa ‘an shimālī, wa min fawqī, wa a‘ūdhu bi-‘aẓamatika an ughtāla min taḥtī.
O Allah, I ask You for pardon and well-being in this world and the next. O Allah, I ask You for pardon and well-being in my religion, my worldly affairs, my family and my wealth. O Allah, conceal my faults and calm my fears. O Allah, guard me from in front of me and behind me, from my right and my left, and from above me; and I seek refuge in Your Greatness from being struck down from beneath me.
Abū Dāwūd 5074; Ibn Mājah 3871 · Book of Du‘a p. 10–11
Sayyid al-Istighfār (the master of seeking forgiveness)
Whoever says it with certainty in the day and dies before evening, or at night and dies before morning, is among the people of Paradise.
ٱللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ رَبِّي لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ، خَلَقْتَنِي وَأَنَا عَبْدُكَ، وَأَنَا عَلَىٰ عَهْدِكَ وَوَعْدِكَ مَا ٱسْتَطَعْتُ، أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا صَنَعْتُ، أَبُوءُ لَكَ بِنِعْمَتِكَ عَلَيَّ، وَأَبُوءُ لَكَ بِذَنْبِي فَٱغْفِرْ لِي، فَإِنَّهُ لَا يَغْفِرُ ٱلذُّنُوبَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ
Allāhumma anta Rabbī lā ilāha illā ant, khalaqtanī wa ana ‘abduk, wa ana ‘alā ‘ahdika wa wa‘dika mastaṭa‘t, a‘ūdhu bika min sharri mā ṣana‘t, abū’u laka bi-ni‘matika ‘alayya, wa abū’u laka bi-dhanbī faghfir lī, fa-innahū lā yaghfirudh-dhunūba illā ant.
O Allah, You are my Lord. There is none worthy of worship besides You. You created me and I am Your servant. I abide by Your covenant and promise to the best of my ability. I seek refuge in You from the evil I have done. I acknowledge Your favours upon me, and I acknowledge my sins, so forgive me, for none forgives sins except You.
Al-Bukhārī 6306 · Book of Du‘a p. 12

Du‘ā’ at 40 years old
The Qur’ān gives this du‘ā’ to the one who reaches full strength and forty years of age.
رَبِّ أَوْزِعْنِىٓ أَنْ أَشْكُرَ نِعْمَتَكَ ٱلَّتِىٓ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَىَّ وَعَلَىٰ وَٰلِدَىَّ وَأَنْ أَعْمَلَ صَٰلِحًا تَرْضَىٰهُ وَأَصْلِحْ لِى فِى ذُرِّيَّتِىٓ ۖ إِنِّى تُبْتُ إِلَيْكَ وَإِنِّى مِنَ ٱلْمُسْلِمِينَ ۝١٥
Rabbi awzi‘nī an ashkura ni‘matakal-latī an‘amta ‘alayya wa ‘alā wālidayya wa an a‘mala ṣāliḥan tarḍāhu wa aṣliḥ lī fī dhurriyyatī, innī tubtu ilayka wa innī minal-muslimīn.
My Lord! Inspire me to (always) be thankful for Your favours which You blessed me and my parents with, and to do good deeds that please You. And instil righteousness in my offspring. I truly repent to You, and I truly submit (to Your Will).

Returning Home

Du‘ā’ upon returning home
Recite the travel du‘ā’ again for the journey home, and add:
آيِبُونَ، تَائِبُونَ، عَابِدُونَ، لِرَبِّنَا حَامِدُونَ
Āyibūna, tā’ibūna, ‘ābidūna, li-Rabbinā ḥāmidūn.
We return, repentant, worshipping, and praising our Lord.
This wording is in Muslim 1342. On the way back from Hajj or Umrah, the Prophet ﷺ would also say takbīr three times on every rise and a fuller form (al-Bukhārī 1797): lā ilāha illallāhu waḥdahū lā sharīka lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-ḥamdu wa huwa ‘alā kulli shay’in qadīr, āyibūna tā’ibūna ‘ābidūna sājidūna li-Rabbinā ḥāmidūn, ṣadaqallāhu wa‘dah, wa naṣara ‘abdah, wa hazamal-aḥzāba waḥdah.
Muslim 1342; al-Bukhārī 1797 · Book of Du‘a p. 16
The recording is the complete travel du‘ā’ (Ḥiṣn al-Muslim 207); say this addition after it.

Salat dua rakaat di rumah sebelum berangkat
Sebelum berangkat, salatlah dua rakaat di rumah. Diriwayatkan bahwa Nabi ﷺ bersabda, tidak ada yang ditinggalkan seseorang untuk keluarganya yang lebih baik daripada dua rakaat yang ia kerjakan di tengah mereka ketika hendak bepergian.
Pilihan yang baik adalah Al-Kafirun pada rakaat pertama dan Al-Ikhlas pada rakaat kedua, setelah Al-Fatihah. Tawaf

Cara memakai doa setiap putaran

Niatkan tawaf tujuh putaran, dengan Ka‘bah di sebelah kiri Anda. Doa-doa berikut, satu untuk setiap putaran, dibaca dari Hajar Aswad sampai Rukun Yamani; antara Rukun Yamani dan Hajar Aswad, setiap putaran ditutup dengan Rabbanā ātinā.

Doa per putaran ini adalah doa susunan ulama yang lazim dalam buku manasik di Indonesia; sunah tidak menetapkan doa khusus untuk setiap putaran. Satu-satunya doa yang diriwayatkan dari Nabi ﷺ dalam tawaf adalah Rabbanā ātinā di antara dua rukun (Abu Dawud 1892, hasan). Bacalah jika membantu Anda, atau mohonlah apa saja kepada Allah dalam bahasa Anda sendiri, dari hati. Pelankan suara agar tidak mengganggu jemaah lain.

Sai antara Shafa dan Marwah

Apa itu Sai? Tata caranya
  • Titik awal: selalu dimulai dari bukit Shafa dan berakhir di Marwah.
  • Menghitung putaran: setiap perjalanan satu arah dihitung satu putaran (Shafa ke Marwah putaran 1, Marwah ke Shafa putaran 2, dan seterusnya), seluruhnya tujuh putaran.
  • Jarak: jarak kedua bukit sekitar 450 meter, sehingga tujuh putaran menempuh sekitar 3,15 km.
  • Lampu hijau: di antara Shafa dan Marwah ada dua penanda lampu neon hijau. Di antara keduanya, laki-laki disunahkan berlari kecil atau berjalan cepat; perempuan tetap berjalan biasa.

Bacalah ayat ini ketika mendekati Shafa; panduan Sai juga mengulanginya ketika mendekati Marwah. Di Shafa, pada kali pertama saja, tambahkan “Aku memulai dengan apa yang Allah mulai.”
إِنَّ ٱلصَّفَا وَٱلْمَرْوَةَ مِن شَعَآئِرِ ٱللَّهِ ۖ فَمَنْ حَجَّ ٱلْبَيْتَ أَوِ ٱعْتَمَرَ فَلَا جُنَاحَ عَلَيْهِ أَن يَطَّوَّفَ بِهِمَا ۚ وَمَن تَطَوَّعَ خَيْرًا فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ شَاكِرٌ عَلِيمٌ ۝١٥٨
أَبْدَأُ بِمَا بَدَأَ ٱللَّهُ بِهِ
Innaṣ-Ṣafā wal-Marwata min sha‘ā’irillāh, fa-man ḥajjal-bayta awi‘tamara falā junāḥa ‘alayhi an yaṭṭawwafa bihimā, wa man taṭawwa‘a khayran fa-innallāha shākirun ‘alīm. Abda’u bimā bada’allāhu bih.
Sesungguhnya Shafa dan Marwah termasuk syiar-syiar Allah. Maka barang siapa beribadah haji ke Baitullah atau berumrah, tidak ada dosa baginya mengerjakan sai antara keduanya. Dan barang siapa mengerjakan kebajikan dengan kerelaan hati, maka sesungguhnya Allah Maha Mensyukuri lagi Maha Mengetahui. Aku memulai dengan apa yang Allah mulai.

Doa Sai (bentuk lengkap)
Zikir yang lebih lengkap dari panduan Sai, untuk dibaca di bukit Shafa dan Marwah:
ٱللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ ٱللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ ٱللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ وَلِلَّهِ ٱلْحَمْدُ. ٱللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ عَلَىٰ مَا هَدَانَا، وَٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ عَلَىٰ مَا أَوْلَانَا. لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ ٱلْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ ٱلْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ. أَنْجَزَ وَعْدَهُ، وَنَصَرَ عَبْدَهُ، وَهَزَمَ ٱلْأَحْزَابَ وَحْدَهُ. لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ وَلَا نَعْبُدُ إِلَّا إِيَّاهُ، مُخْلِصِينَ لَهُ ٱلدِّينَ وَلَوْ كَرِهَ ٱلْكَافِرُونَ. وَٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَالَمِينَ
Allāhu akbar, Allāhu akbar, Allāhu akbar, wa lillāhil-ḥamd. Allāhu akbaru ‘alā mā hadānā, wal-ḥamdu lillāhi ‘alā mā awlānā. Lā ilāha illallāhu waḥdahū lā sharīka lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-ḥamdu wa huwa ‘alā kulli shay’in qadīr. Anjaza wa‘dah, wa naṣara ‘abdah, wa hazamal-aḥzāba waḥdah. Lā ilāha illallāhu wa lā na‘budu illā iyyāh, mukhliṣīna lahud-dīna wa law karihal-kāfirūn. Wal-ḥamdu lillāhi Rabbil-‘ālamīn.
Allah Mahabesar, Allah Mahabesar, Allah Mahabesar, dan bagi Allah segala puji. Allah Mahabesar atas petunjuk yang diberikan-Nya kepada kami, dan segala puji bagi Allah atas karunia yang dilimpahkan-Nya kepada kami. Tiada tuhan selain Allah semata, tiada sekutu bagi-Nya; milik-Nya kerajaan dan segala puji, dan Dia Mahakuasa atas segala sesuatu. Dia menepati janji-Nya, menolong hamba-Nya, dan mengalahkan pasukan sekutu sendirian. Tiada tuhan selain Allah, dan kami tidak menyembah selain kepada-Nya, dengan memurnikan ketaatan kepada-Nya, walaupun orang-orang kafir tidak menyukainya. Dan segala puji bagi Allah, Tuhan semesta alam.

Di antara dua lampu hijau
Laki-laki berlari kecil di antara dua lampu hijau; perempuan berjalan biasa. Bacalah:
رَبِّ ٱغْفِرْ وَٱرْحَمْ، وَٱعْفُ وَتَكَرَّمْ، وَتَجَاوَزْ عَمَّا تَعْلَمُ، إِنَّكَ تَعْلَمُ مَا لَا نَعْلَمُ. ٱللَّهُمَّ ٱغْفِرْ وَٱرْحَمْ، إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ ٱلْأَعَزُّ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ. رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي ٱلدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي ٱلْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ ٱلنَّارِ
Rabbighfir warḥam, wa‘fu wa takarram, wa tajāwaz ‘ammā ta‘lam, innaka ta‘lamu mā lā na‘lam. Allāhummaghfir warḥam, innaka antal-a‘azzul-akram. Rabbanā ātinā fid-dunyā ḥasanatan wa fil-ākhirati ḥasanatan wa qinā ‘adhāban-nār.
Ya Tuhanku, ampunilah dan rahmatilah, maafkanlah dan muliakanlah, dan lewatkanlah apa yang Engkau ketahui (dari dosa-dosaku); sesungguhnya Engkau mengetahui apa yang tidak kami ketahui. Ya Allah, ampunilah dan rahmatilah; sesungguhnya Engkau Yang Mahaperkasa lagi Mahamulia. Ya Tuhan kami, berilah kami kebaikan di dunia dan kebaikan di akhirat, dan lindungilah kami dari azab neraka.
Kalimat intinya, Rabbighfir warḥam, innaka antal-a‘azzul-akram, diriwayatkan dari ‘Abdullah bin Mas‘ud dan Ibnu ‘Umar ketika sai (Mushannaf Ibnu Abi Syaibah). Tambahan kalimat lainnya terdapat dalam kitab-kitab doa yang lebih kemudian.

Doa setelah Sai
Setelah putaran ketujuh, yang berakhir di Marwah:
ٱللَّهُمَّ رَبَّنَا تَقَبَّلْ مِنَّا، وَعَافِنَا وَٱعْفُ عَنَّا، وَعَلَىٰ طَاعَتِكَ وَشُكْرِكَ أَعِنَّا، وَعَلَىٰ غَيْرِكَ لَا تَكِلْنَا، وَعَلَى ٱلْإِيمَانِ وَٱلْإِسْلَامِ ٱلْكَامِلِ جَمِيعًا تَوَفَّنَا وَأَنْتَ رَاضٍ عَنَّا. ٱللَّهُمَّ ٱرْحَمْنِي بِتَرْكِ ٱلْمَعَاصِي أَبَدًا مَا أَبْقَيْتَنِي، وَٱرْحَمْنِي أَنْ أَتَكَلَّفَ مَا لَا يَعْنِينِي، وَٱرْزُقْنِي حُسْنَ ٱلنَّظَرِ فِيمَا يُرْضِيكَ عَنِّي، يَا أَرْحَمَ ٱلرَّاحِمِينَ
Allāhumma Rabbanā taqabbal minnā, wa ‘āfinā wa‘fu ‘annā, wa ‘alā ṭā‘atika wa shukrika a‘innā, wa ‘alā ghayrika lā takilnā, wa ‘alal-īmāni wal-islāmil-kāmili jamī‘an tawaffanā wa anta rāḍin ‘annā. Allāhummarḥamnī bi-tarkil-ma‘āṣī abadan mā abqaytanī, warḥamnī an atakallafa mā lā ya‘nīnī, warzuqnī ḥusnan-naẓari fīmā yurḍīka ‘annī, yā Arḥamar-rāḥimīn.
Ya Allah, Tuhan kami, terimalah (amal) dari kami, berilah kami kesehatan dan maafkanlah kami; tolonglah kami untuk taat dan bersyukur kepada-Mu; janganlah Engkau serahkan kami kepada selain-Mu; dan wafatkanlah kami semua di atas iman dan Islam yang sempurna dalam keadaan Engkau rida kepada kami. Ya Allah, rahmatilah aku dengan meninggalkan maksiat selamanya selama Engkau menghidupkanku; rahmatilah aku agar tidak membebani diri dengan apa yang tidak bermanfaat bagiku; dan karuniakanlah kepadaku pandangan yang baik terhadap apa yang membuat-Mu rida kepadaku, wahai Yang Maha Pengasih di antara para pengasih.
Untuk umrah, sai dilanjutkan dengan mencukur atau memotong rambut (tahalul).

آيِبُونَ، تَائِبُونَ، عَابِدُونَ، لِرَبِّنَا حَامِدُونَ
Āyibūna, tā’ibūna, ‘ābidūna, li-Rabbinā ḥāmidūn.
Kami kembali, bertobat, beribadah, dan memuji Tuhan kami.
Lafaz ini terdapat dalam Muslim 1342.
